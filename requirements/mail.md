# Mail requirements

## M-1 List

Show the threads in the inbox, split into unread and read, each sorted newest first. The unread rows have a heading above them (`#unread-heading`, in the scrolling area, the pair of the heading "既読" above the read rows): "未読" and the number of unread threads of the list at the left and M-6's "すべて既読にする" at the right; with no unread thread the heading is not shown. The list has a header (`#inbox-header`, fixed above the list in the tint of the thread bar, shown on the list only: the title "受信トレイ" only) and no reload button except the one of a failed load below (the bar `#mail-header` is shown only in a thread). Above the rows, `#inbox-state` (in the scrolling area, hidden unless the list is shown) shows the state of the list in three ways: while the first load has not answered and no row is drawn, a spinner with "読み込んでいます…" (the list is shown at startup for this, with the kept list's rows when there is one); when a load answered and there is no row, "受信トレイにスレッドはありません"; and when the last load failed (the call, or an account in its answer), one line "読み込めませんでした: {the error, or each failed account and its error}。自動でも読み直します" with the button "再読み込み", the only time the button is there. The button loads the list at once, and it cannot be pressed while a load is on its way; when rows are drawn they stay under the line. The failure belongs to the list and not to the error line `#error`, so it stays when another screen is shown and the list comes back, and it goes when a load succeeds with no failed account; only the latest load decides it. A load that failed (the call, or an account in its answer) is repeated by the sync round, which runs every 10 seconds and when the window is shown again, once the wait has passed since that load (60 seconds, doubled after each failed repeat up to 5 minutes), until a load succeeds with no failed account, which clears the wait; when a sync answers that an account needs a login while the list is not shown (a thread is open), the list is loaded again at the next sync round; a load never switches the screen; a repeated load clears only the notice the inbox showed itself in `#error` (the one of repeated failures of the sync), and keeps its failure for the list whichever screen is shown; a load that draws the rows again keeps the focus on the same button of the same row

## M-2 Read

Open a thread and show its body. Pressing a row of the list (or "詳細を開く" of M-9, or a task's mail link) goes to the detail view at once and shows the subject and the sender that the row has (a thread opened from a task with no row shows neither), and in the body's place the note "読み込んでいます…" with gray lines; when the thread arrives they are replaced by the messages. Until it arrives, the buttons of the row of operations (below: the AI summary, Slack, Todo, Orca, trash and archive) cannot be pressed, and "戻る" of the header goes back to where the thread was opened from. A press on another row or on "戻る" while it loads leaves the earlier answer ignored: it never draws over the screen then shown. When the call fails, the same error text as before is shown in the body's place (and the buttons stay disabled). When the answer says the account must log in again, the list is shown. A thread that was opened before in this run is drawn at once from the copy the app holds of Gmail's last answer for it, with no note and with the buttons enabled as for a thread that arrived, and Gmail is asked again at the same time. When the answer draws the same (the subject, the messages and their images), nothing is drawn again, so a body keeps its place and its frames do not load again; when it differs (a new reply, say), the thread is drawn again from the answer. When that call fails, the copy stays readable and the error is shown where the mail screen shows its errors (`#error`), not in the body's place; when the answer says the account must log in again, the list is shown as above. Whether the thread is marked read (M-6) follows Gmail's answer, not the copy. The copies are in memory only, never in a file, and are gone when the app quits; they take at most 64 MB (`HELD_THREAD_BYTES`), the oldest dropped first, and a thread larger than that is not held. The copies of an account are dropped when the account is removed, and when Gmail answers that it must log in again. A thread that is not held is waited for as described above. Why. The owner asked for a faster mail screen: every open, the reopen of the same thread included, waited for Gmail behind the note (issue #614). The header of the open thread (`#mail-header`, as high as every band, A-23) has "戻る" (`#back`) only. The operations of the open thread are a row of icon buttons (`#thread-actions`, the first child of `#thread`, just above the subject) in this order: the AI summary (`#thread-summary`, shown only while Claude can be used), "Slack に貼る" (`#thread-slack`), "Todo にする" (`#thread-todo`), "Orca に渡す" (`#thread-orca`, A-9), a thin vertical line, and "ゴミ箱へ移動" (`#trash`) and "アーカイブ" (`#archive`) (the line is not drawn where those two are hidden, a thread opened from a task with no row). Each is a `.icon-btn` of 34px with an icon of 20px, with its `aria-label` as the tooltip, and the buttons sit at the row's right end, the right end of the body (the owner's instruction, 2026-11-13, issue #529). The row stays at the top of the scrolling area (`position: sticky`) while the messages scroll, in the color of the body so that nothing shows through it, and it is within the area at the left of the Orca pane (A-9) when that is open. Why. The owner chose this layout (issue #503, option A): the buttons stayed far from the subject, at the right end of the bar, and the bar is now only "戻る". The owner then asked for the buttons at the right end of their own row (issue #529). The body is drawn as Gmail draws it. An HTML body that names no font draws in `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` at 13px: a rule of the frame's root and body ahead of the mail's own styles, so a font the mail names (its CSS, an inline style, `<font face>`) wins, and so does a size it names on the body or inside it (a size named only on the root does not, because the body's 13px is set under it). A text body (`.plain`) draws in the same sans-serif, not a monospace one, at 13px; with a font chosen for the mail screen (A-20) it takes that family and size, while an HTML body keeps its own fonts (A-20). Line breaks and wrapping of a text body are as they were. The http(s) URL that stands as text in an HTML body, outside a link, is a link too: the text nodes of the frame's document (not those inside `a`, `script`, `style`, `textarea`, `title`, `svg`, `math`, `select`, `button`, `noframes`, `noembed`, `xmp`, `plaintext`) are searched for URLs by the rule of a text body (a URL ends at whitespace, a non-ASCII character or `"` `<` `>`, trailing punctuation is left out, and a trailing `)` only when unpaired), and each is wrapped in an `<a href>` built with DOM calls (the mail's text is never put in as markup). It then goes the way of every link (`routeLinks`: `target="_top"` and the URL as the title, opened in the default browser by Rust), and nothing else of the frame's sandbox and CSP changes. Text that is not an http(s) URL is left as text. A URL that another element cuts (a `<wbr>`, a `<span>`) is a link up to the cut, because each text node is searched on its own. Why. The owner reported that a mail drew in a face unlike Gmail's, and that a URL in the text of an HTML body could not be pressed (issues #494 and #495, parts of #493).

## M-6 Mark read

Mark a thread read or unread with the checkbox in the list. The button "すべて既読にする" at the right end of the heading "未読" above the unread rows (M-1; the heading, and so the button, is not shown while no thread is unread; the button is disabled while a run is on its way) makes every unread thread in the list (the ones shown, not unread threads that are not on the screen) read with the existing `set_read`, one request per thread, at most 4 at once: the rows change first, a row whose request fails (or answers that the account must log in again) goes back to unread and the failures are reported once, and a read box stays disabled while its request is on its way so that a sync leaves the row as it is (as for a single toggle). When it ends, the notice "N 件を既読にしました" offers "元に戻す", which makes the threads it changed (those still in the list) unread again the same way

## M-7 Auto sync

Sync the inbox with Gmail about every 10 seconds

## M-8 AI summary

In the mail detail, an icon summarizes the thread with the Claude Code CLI (`claude -p`, the model and effort of A-2 with sonnet and medium as the default, no tools, no MCP, no settings) and shows a short list of points, in Japanese, on a card under the subject. Only pressing the icon sends the subject and, of each message, the sender, the date and the text (newest first, up to 10,000 characters, the oldest dropped first). The summary is kept in memory per thread (and its latest message), never saved, and shown again without sending when the card is closed and reopened. The icon is shown only while Claude can be used (logged in, or an API key that is not refused, A-4)

## M-9 New mail popup

When auto sync (M-7) finds new mail, show a popup for each new thread at the bottom right of the app window, so that the mail can be handled at once. Nothing else shows it: the load at startup, a load or a reload after the history was too old, and a sync that only changes labels (an undo of a trash or an archive, a mark as read) show none, and no OS notification is used. A thread is new when the sync finds a message added to the inbox with the UNREAD label and the thread is still unread and in the inbox when it is fetched (Rust decides it and `sync` answers the IDs in `arrived`).

The popup shows the sender and the subject, and the actions "ゴミ箱へ移動" (M-3) and "アーカイブ" (M-4) as icon buttons, "詳細を開く" (M-2) as a text button, and a close button. Trash and archive call the same commands as the list row (`removeRow`), so the row leaves the list at once, the notice with "元に戻す" shows, and a failure brings the row back and shows the error. "詳細を開く" goes to the mail screen and opens the thread, even from another screen. The sync alone never changes the screen (`frontend.md`): the popup only appears over the current one. Each action closes the popup.

Details the owner left open:

- Several new threads at once: one card each, stacked, the newest at the bottom. At most 3 cards show, counting the popups for new Slack messages (S-17), which share this stack; a fourth makes the oldest go (its thread stays in the list, unread).
- A card goes by itself after 10 seconds. The pointer or the focus on it holds it, and the 10 seconds start again when they leave. The close button closes it at once.
- A thread whose card is on screen gets no second card, and neither does the thread that is open on screen (or that the detail view is still loading). A card goes when its thread is opened, from the list or with "詳細を開く", so that no button of it acts on a thread the detail view holds.
- A card goes when its thread becomes read, whether the owner marks it read in the list (the checkbox of a row, "すべて既読にする") or a sync finds it read (read in Gmail or on another device), so that a card never stays for a thread that needs no attention.
- The cards stack above the toast, which is above the update popup.
