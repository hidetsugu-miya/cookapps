# Calendar requirements

## C-1 View

Show the events of Google Calendar (read-only).

The bar above the week (`#cal-bar`) is the tinted band of the header of the Todo screen: `--soft` with a 2px underline in `--accent`, at least `--header-h` high. The heading row of the panel (C-4) is the same band at the same height, so that the two lines meet. The bar holds, from the left, the button 「今日」 (shows the week that holds today), the arrows ‹ › (the week before and the week after), and the month of the week as a button, written as 「2026年11月」: the year and the month of the middle day of the week, run together, in one weight and one color. While the panel is closed, the button that opens it is first in the bar. The month button opens the month calendar of C-4 (below). In the header of the days, today's date is a circle in `--accent`.

## C-2 Event detail

Press an event of the week (timed or all-day) and a popover beside it shows the color dot, the title, the time, a "Meet に参加" button with the URL and a copy button (only for a `https://meet.google.com/` link, from `hangoutLink`), the place, the description (HTML turned into text, links kept), the number of invited people (rooms left out) with how many accepted, the calendar and account, and the user's own answer. Nothing is fetched on a press: the detail comes with the week and is kept with it in `calendar-cache.json` (no name or address of an attendee is fetched). Another event switches it, the same one, ✕, Escape, a click elsewhere, a scroll, a resize, a week change or a screen change closes it, and a background redraw of the week keeps it open on the same event

## C-3 First day of the week

The settings screen has a "カレンダー" tab where the first day of the week is chosen from Sunday to Saturday (the default is Sunday). It is saved in `calendar-settings.json` in the app config directory and the calendar draws its weeks from that day

## C-4 Calendars to show

The calendar screen has a panel, 248px wide, left of the week, headed "カレンダー". Above the calendars is a month calendar (below). The calendars are grouped by account. The heading of a group is a row of the account's avatar (the same initials and color as its avatar in the sidebar), its address and a chevron; pressing the row folds the account's calendars away and shows them again (`aria-expanded`; which accounts are folded is held in memory only, so every account is open when the app starts, and no file is added). Under the row the calendars are indented and held by a line (`--line`, 1px) at their left, and the space between accounts is clear. The calendars are in two parts, "マイカレンダー" (the calendars the account owns) and "他のカレンダー" (the others: a person's calendar added to the list, holidays, rooms), each as a checkbox and the calendar's name. The checkbox takes the calendar's color (the account's color when the calendar has none), and a part with no calendar is not drawn. Checking or unchecking a calendar saves the choice in `calendar-selection.json` in the app config directory, writes nothing to Google (the scope stays `calendar.readonly`), and draws the week again from the new choice (once, 300 milliseconds after the last change of a run of them); the checkbox is disabled while its choice is being saved, and a choice that cannot be saved puts the checkbox back and shows the error. A saved change also deletes `calendar-cache.json`, so the weeks kept for the next start never hold a hidden calendar's events, and removing an account removes its choices from `calendar-selection.json`. A calendar never chosen follows `selected` at Google. The panel is read when the calendar is opened and again when the week is refreshed or the accounts change, and it is drawn again only when what was read differs from what is drawn. A button beside the heading closes the panel and the week takes its width; while it is closed the same button is first in the bar above the week, and pressing it opens the panel again. The panel is open when the app starts, and whether it is closed is held in memory only

The month calendar. It is a small calendar of one month: its title 「2026年11月」 with ‹ › that move the month (`前の月`, `次の月`), the weekdays in a row, and the days in a row for each week. The weekdays start on the first day of the week of C-3. The row of the week shown is tinted (`--soft`), today is a circle in `--accent`, and the days of the months before and after are lighter. Pressing a day shows the week that holds it, through the same move as the arrows of the bar. It is at the top of the panel, outside the part that scrolls, and the month of the title of the bar opens the same calendar under the bar, over the week, so that a day can be chosen while the panel is closed too; pressing a day there moves to its week and closes it, and Escape, a press elsewhere and the month button again close it. The month shown follows the week drawn (the month of its middle day, as the title of the bar says) and is moved by the arrows of either calendar, which show the same month. The days are made by `calendar_month(year, month, firstDay)` of Rust (`month_grid` in `calendar.rs`: the weeks that hold the month, each of seven days from the first day of the week, answered with the weekday of each column), so that no date arithmetic is in the page, and nothing is saved.
